American Solar Challenge

About

After watching the movie ‘Race the sun’, where a handful of high school kids built a car on solar power, a dream was planted in the heads of a group of TU Delft students.

About Delft Solar Team

Nuna 13S

Team Nuna 13S is ready to race in the Electrek American Solar Challenge. A completely new continent. An unknown route across the United States. And a challenge no Dutch team has taken on before.

Meet the team

Partners

Our partners are the absolute key to make the dream come true. Reaching sustainability is not done by individuals, but by collaborations.

Get to know our partners

Become a partner

We are always open for new partnerships and collaborations. Do you see synergies between your company's philosophy and our team? Don’t hesitate to contact us and let’s sit around the table to fantasize about the ideal partnership!

Become a part of the Nuna family

Events

During our years of solar racing, we have built quite some experience. We are eager to tell you all our secrets behind our success and overcoming obstacles. Think about a workshop in teambuilding or attending your company’s event with our precious solar car, Nuna!

Nuna at your event

Green Actions

At Delft Solar Team, our mission goes beyond building solar cars. We aim to spark real, lasting change. That’s why we not only inspire our partners but also encourage them to take action in the field of sustainability.
